Cláusulas adverbiales

Spanish adverbial clauses · practice you can write on and keep

Learn when to use the indicative or the subjunctive after time, purpose, condition, cause, and concession conjunctions — then apply it to sustainability topics. Explanations are in English; every example and exercise is in Spanish.

You can write directly on these pages. Your answers save automatically in the browser on the device you are using. They are not sent to anyone and they are not stored online, so if you switch devices or clear your browsing data they do not follow you. For a copy that lasts, use Imprimir / Guardar en PDF (choose “Save as PDF”) or Descargar mis respuestas at the top of each page. Prefer paper? Print any page blank and write by hand.
